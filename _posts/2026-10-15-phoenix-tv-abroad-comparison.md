# Watch Phoenix TV abroad: M3U vs Sling vs KyLinTV

> Audience: Overseas Chinese who want to keep up with Phoenix TV (Phoenix Info News / Phoenix Chinese Channel). This article compares three real paths, with the real prices from each service. It does not endorse any specific service.

---

## 1. The problem: Phoenix TV is hard to find abroad

Phoenix TV (凤凰卫视) has two channels that Chinese-speaking audiences look for:

- **Phoenix Info News** (凤凰卫视资讯台, HD): 24-hour Chinese news, Hong Kong-based
- **Phoenix Chinese Channel** (凤凰卫视中文台): Chinese-language entertainment, dramas, talk shows

If you are a Hong Kong immigrant in Toronto, a businessperson in London, or a student in Sydney, here is the cold reality:

- **Not on local cable or streaming bundles**: not on US Hulu+ Live TV, YouTube TV, Sling TV mainstream packages, not on Canada Bell / Rogers, not on UK Sky TV mainstream packages, not on Australia Foxtel.
- **Sling TV only has Phoenix Info News** — and it requires the **$20/month "Heartland" or similar extra add-on**, **plus** the mainstream package (Orange $40 or Blue $40, base price ~$60/month total).
- **DirecTV only carries Phoenix Info News** — also requires the **$20/month "Heartland" Chinese/Asian add-on** (DirecTV's general international package, not included in the base plan).
- **Neither official app has a stable overseas live experience**: Phoenix Now app's overseas experience is unstable; some regions cannot even register an account.

A few **factual statements** without judging which is better:

1. **M3U (*.m3u / *.m3u8) is a public playlist file format**. **The format itself is just a technical specification and does not bind to any specific channel or content source.** Whether an M3U subscription service is legitimate depends on whether its content source is authorized.
2. **Sling International / Heartland package page** (sling.com) clearly states these add-ons are intended for "international viewers" and include Phoenix Info News — but **only Phoenix Info News**, not Phoenix Chinese Channel. For people who specifically want Phoenix Chinese Channel, this path is incomplete.
3. **DirecTV international package** is only available in the US; **it cannot be subscribed in Canada, UK, or Australia**.

## 3. The technical path: Sling (US) + M3U (worldwide) combination

The most realistic 2026 overseas Phoenix TV setup is **a combination**: use Sling TV for legal, stable Phoenix Info News in the US; use an M3U subscription for the rest of the world (or for Phoenix Chinese Channel).

### Path A: Sling TV (US, simplest, legal)

For US residents who mainly watch Phoenix Info News:

1. Go to sling.com, register an account, subscribe to **Orange $40/month or Blue $40/month** (or a combo plan).
2. Add the **"Heartland" or "International" package** (about $20/month extra) at checkout.
3. Search "Phoenix" in the channel list; Phoenix Info News will show up.
4. Watch on Fire TV / Apple TV / Roku / Smart TV / phone / tablet.

**This path does NOT give you Phoenix Chinese Channel.** If your parents specifically want Phoenix Chinese Channel, you have to add a second subscription.

### Path B: M3U subscription (worldwide, cheapest)

For non-US regions, or people who want both Phoenix channels:

#### Hardware

- **Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K Max**: Amazon US listing **$89.48** (one-time).
- **Any Android TV smart TV**: works.
- **NVIDIA Shield TV Pro**: about $199, for 4K HDR households.

#### Software: M3U player

**An M3U player only reads the playlist, displays the EPG, and sends playback requests.** It does not provide any content itself.

| Platform | Recommended player | Notes |
|----------|-------------------|-------|
| Android TV / Fire TV / Shield | **TiviMate** | UI closest to traditional cable TV EPG; tivimate.com explicitly states "TiviMate is a media player only and does not provide any content" |
| Android TV / Fire TV / Shield | **Televizo** | Google Play 4.0 rating / 17.7K reviews (com.ottplay.ottplay); modern UI, multiple playlists; free version works, PRO subscription unlocks 5 devices / ad-free |
| iOS / iPadOS / Apple TV | **Smarters IPTV Player** | Listed on the App Store, developer Smarters; also only reads M3U links, no content |
| iOS / iPadOS / Apple TV | **APTV** | Listed on the App Store (id 1630403500), developer Kimen; supports iPhone / iPad / Mac / Apple TV / Apple Watch / Vision Pro across six Apple platforms + CarPlay video casting |
| Any platform | **VLC media player** | Free and open source, supports M3U; but does not render EPG guide, you need to organize channels yourself |

#### How to import an M3U link into the player

The configuration flow is the same for any of the players above:

1. **Prepare an M3U subscription link** — usually a URL you get from a paid subscription via email or backend, shaped like `https://example.com/sub/your-code.m3u`. **Only subscribe to a service that explicitly lists "Phoenix Info News" AND "Phoenix Chinese" in its channel lineup** — search "Phoenix" in the provider's channel list before purchase.
2. **Paste the link into the player's "Add playlist from URL" entry point**:
   - TiviMate: Settings → My Playlists → Add playlist from URL
   - Televizo: top-left menu → Playlists → ➕ → Enter URL
   - Smarters IPTV Player: bottom Login → Add User via M3U URL
   - APTV: bottom Sources → Add Source → M3U URL
3. **Wait for the list to load** (usually 10-30 seconds); search "Phoenix" and add both Phoenix Info News and Phoenix Chinese to favorites.
4. **Test during peak hours**: try a Phoenix news bulletin at 7-9 PM China time — peak traffic reveals the provider's real service quality.

## 4. FAQ

**Q1: Does Sling TV give me both Phoenix Info News and Phoenix Chinese Channel?**

A: **No.** Sling's international add-on ($20/month) only includes Phoenix Info News, not Phoenix Chinese Channel. If you specifically want the Chinese-language channel, you need a second subscription (M3U subscription).

**Q2: How much does KyLinTV cost, and does it have Phoenix TV?**

A: **KyLinTV North America** is $16.99/month for the base pack, $22.99/month for the China TV / Taiwan TV pack. The official page does NOT explicitly list Phoenix TV. Confirm with KyLinTV support before purchase.

**Q3: Can I watch Phoenix TV on iPhone / iPad / Apple TV?**

A: Yes, but TiviMate has no iOS version. iOS / iPadOS / Apple TV users can use **Smarters IPTV Player** or **APTV** (App Store id 1630403500, by Kimen), or **VLC** — pick any one and import the same M3U link. **Do not try to install "TiviMate alternatives" on iOS** — App Store review is strict; anything that gets listed only reads M3U and has no built-in content.

**Q4: I am in the UK / Australia / Canada, can I subscribe to Sling / DirecTV?**

A: Sling TV and DirecTV are both US-only services. UK / Australia / Canada residents can only use the M3U subscription path.

**Q5: Is using an M3U subscription to watch Phoenix TV legal?**

A: M3U is a public playlist file format — **it does not bind to any specific content.** Whether your personal viewing is legal depends on whether the M3U link's content has been authorized by the rights holder. **This article does not evaluate any specific service's content source.** The simplest way to avoid risk is to use only official, authorized subscriptions (such as Sling's international add-on) — but that path only has Phoenix Info News, not Phoenix Chinese Channel.

## 5. Conclusion

For Phoenix TV overseas, **the most realistic 2026 setup is**:

- **US residents who only watch Phoenix Info News**: Sling TV + International add-on (~$60/month total)
- **US residents who want both channels, or non-US residents**: M3U subscription ($5-10/month) + Fire TV Stick 4K Max ($89.48, one-time)
- **DirecTV**: works but is more expensive (~$100-120/month) and only has Phoenix Info News

Public GitHub lists are for ad-hoc testing only. KyLinTV does not explicitly include Phoenix — confirm before subscribing.
